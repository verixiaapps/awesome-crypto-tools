## Price Tracking & Market Data

- [2Bitcoins](https://2bitcoins.com) — Live Bitcoin and crypto prices with exchange reviews, trading tools, and educational guides.
- [CoinGecko](https://coingecko.com) — Comprehensive price tracking for 10,000+ coins with developer API.
- [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com) — Widely used crypto market cap rankings and price data.
- [TradingView](https://tradingview.com) — Advanced charting platform with crypto pairs across all major exchanges.
- [Messari](https://messari.io) — Crypto market intelligence with curated data and research.
- [CryptoCompare](https://cryptocompare.com) — Real-time and historical crypto data with exchange reviews.
- [LiveCoinWatch](https://livecoinwatch.com) — Real-time price tracking with portfolio features.
- [Nomics](https://nomics.com) — Clean, institutional-grade market data API.

## Derivatives & Perpetual Futures

- [PerpFinder](https://perpfinder.com) — Compare perpetual futures exchanges by volume, fees, open interest, TVL, and funding rates.
- [CoinGlass](https://coinglass.com) — Futures data including open interest, liquidations, funding rates, and long/short ratios.
- [Coinalyze](https://coinalyze.net) — Advanced futures analytics with aggregated open interest and funding data.
- [Laevitas](https://laevitas.ch) — Derivatives analytics covering perps, options, and volatility surfaces.
- [DefiLlama Derivatives](https://defillama.com/derivatives) — DEX derivatives volume and TVL tracking.
- [Velo Data](https://velo.xyz) — Institutional-grade crypto derivatives data.
